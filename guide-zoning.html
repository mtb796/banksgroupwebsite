TO BUILD — BY-RIGHT

Single-family: meet the district min area + width, hold 25 ft front/rear + combined side yards, stay under coverage & ~3 stories → building permit (no site plan for detached).

2–6 units (EHO): lot in R-5→R-20, not in a GLUP planning district, wide enough for the product → administrative permit, not a rezoning.

Near water: delineate the RPA 100-ft buffer first — it comes out of the buildable envelope before setbacks.

TO SUBDIVIDE — LOT-BY-LOT TEST

Each new lot must independently meet the district's min area and width, plus street frontage.

Max lots ≈ area ÷ min lot area (R-5 every 5,000 SF, R-10 every 10,000, …), reality-checked against width, frontage, and setbacks.

Combine with EHO to subdivide and put 2–6 units on each lot — the Cedar Ridge play.

Planned districts trade lot lines for flexibility: PDH (density by subdistrict; $2,520/unit on recreation), PRC (≥ 750 ac, population-based, ≤ 13 persons/ac), PRM (40+ du/ac or 20+ mixed, 20% open), PCC (aging-in-place, FAR 0.40–1.25+). Also R-C (watershed), R-MHP (manufactured, 10/ac). ADU/WDU density bonuses apply R-2 → R-30.

TO BUILD — BY-RIGHT (SFD)

Single-family detached meeting min lot / setbacks / 35 ft is exempt from site plan — building permit only.

Occupancy needs a Residential Use Permit: pass inspections, final-grade + seed/sod, streets to base asphalt, as-built plat within 30 days.

Every plan requires RPA & RMA delineation — the 100-ft buffer is carved out before density counts.

TO SUBDIVIDE — CONVENTIONAL / REZONE

Conventional: each lot ≥ the conventional minimum; shape-factor rules block flag lots. Lots ≈ area ÷ conv. minimum.

Cluster: smaller lots if the tract meets the district's minimum acreage and you dedicate the open-space %.

To raise density (rezone): Generalized Development Plan + stormwater plan + public notice → Planning Commission + Board of Supervisors. Months, not weeks.
